# 0013. Weekly sets per muscle set by the evidence: 10 to 20, goal lifts keep 3

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-13
- **Deciders:** David ("if so let's update it"); evidence review run by session tweener
- **Tenet invoked:** 2 (evidence before novelty) and 3 (technique before time)

## Context

David asked three things on 10/13: whether 4 working sets a week per muscle is enough, whether
every lift can drop from 3 sets to 2 to save time, and whether heavy weights taken close to
failure give both size and strength. Block 1 ran about 13 chest, 18 back, 17 quad, 9 hamstring
and 7 side-delt hard sets a week, with machine rests of 2 minutes.

## Options considered

- **4 sets a week per muscle.** The minimum effective dose (Pelland et al. 2026, Sports Med:
  the first detectable gain); roughly half the growth of 16 sets on the paper's best-fit curve.
  Enough to keep muscle, not to build it as fast as he wants.
- **Every lift to 2 sets.** Saves about 7 to 10 minutes a session; removes about 30% of weekly
  volume and drops chest, arms and side delts under the useful range. Weekly total per muscle is
  what drives growth, not sets per exercise.
- **Selective cuts to the 10 to 20 range, plus shorter machine rests.** Saves about 2.5 to 3
  minutes a session from sets and 1 to 2 more from rest, with every muscle still in range.

## Decision

The third. Aim for 10 to 20 fractional sets a week per muscle (about 12 to 16 on the priority
muscles). Changes: machine chest press 3 to 2; Push A lateral raise 3 to 4; chest-supported high
row 3 to 2; Pull B pull-up 4 to 3; Pull B lat pulldown 3 to 2; Bulgarian split squat 3 to 2;
Legs B squat 3 to 2; Legs B seated leg curl 3 to 4. Machine rests 120 to 90 seconds (Singer et
al. 2024: no detectable size benefit past 90 s). Goal lifts keep 3 sets: incline DB press, heavy
squat, flat bench, overhead press, the heavy row. Effort rules unchanged: heavy compounds at 1 to
2 reps in reserve, isolation last set to failure (Refalo 2023, 2024: failure adds a trivial
amount of growth and real fatigue; Schoenfeld 2017: heavy loads win strength, size is similar
across loads near failure). Sources are under each lift (P13).

## Consequences

Sessions run about 4 to 5 minutes shorter on average and every muscle stays where growth is
efficient. What gets worse: back drops from 18 to 14 direct sets, and the shorter machine rests
may cost a rep on those sets.
