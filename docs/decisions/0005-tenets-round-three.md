# 0005. Seven ordered tenets for Anvil v2

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-12
- **Deciders:** David; drafted by session tweener, cross-reviewed by wallfacer
- **Tenet invoked:** none (this record sets the tenets)

## Context

Anvil's v1 doctrine had a job and prohibitions but no tenets, so nothing broke ties. David's 2026-10-12 brief: an expert trainer and a daily routine on one page; a plan that covers the whole body plus one conditioning session and mobility; every exercise backed by a study; good form inside a 60-minute session; pushy reminders; consistency over perfection. The reasoning is in Crystal `Lanes/App-Doctrines-2026-10-12`.

## Options considered

- **No tenets, prohibitions only.** Nothing to break a tie between, say, a short session and a complete plan.
- **A flat set.** Leaves coverage against time to whoever argues hardest.
- **An ordered stack.** Coverage outranks evidence outranks time, decided before the argument.

## Decision

The ordered seven in `DOCTRINE.md`: the plan covers the goal and the whole body; evidence before novelty; technique before time; attendance before perfection; recording costs less than skipping and the app persists; one page per day; coach, not counter. Vetted sources to confirm before any is cited in the app: Brad Schoenfeld (confirmed 2026-10-12), Eric Helms, Greg Nuckols, Mike Israetel, Jeff Nippard. Four new Core Purpose promises (P12 to P15) carry the tenets into done tests.

## Consequences

A plan with a gap is not issued, even when the person would rather skip a muscle group. What gets worse: every exercise now needs a source and a cue before it can enter the programme, which slows change.
