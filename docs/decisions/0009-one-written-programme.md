# 0009. One written programme; the tier control is cut

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-11
- **Deciders:** David; session zermatt
- **Tenet invoked:** 4 (attendance before perfection)

## Context

Sessions carried three tiers, a control on the session screen. David committed to one written programme (Crystal `Life/Training-Recipe`) and said the app only needs one page.

## Options considered

- **Keep three tiers.** Flexible on a bad day; one more decision at 6:00 am.
- **One programme, with lifts marked first cut and one main lift per day.** No choice to make; a short day still counts.

## Decision

The tier control is gone. Each session is the session as written; lifts are marked **first cut** for a short day and one **main lift** per day is never cut. A partial day advances the queue like a full one.

## Consequences

Core Purpose P3 keeps its promise with a new mechanism. What gets worse: a very bad day has no pre-priced minimum session.
